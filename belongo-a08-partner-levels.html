What each level can do

PendingJust approved and on probation. Can add listings, but every new listing and every price change is reviewed by Belongo before it goes live.
ApprovedIdentity confirmed and manually approved. Manages listings in one assigned region. New listings are still checked by Belongo before publishing.
Trusted CuratorA strong quality record. Publishes listings directly, with spot checks by Belongo, and has priority support.
Regional LeaderPhase 4, not live. Supervises several partners, helps onboard new ones and watches quality in a region.
